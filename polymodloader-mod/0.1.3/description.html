Replays a PolyTrack recording on your own car, with the exact inputs tick by tick. Press Replay champion to fetch the current map's champion through the local server (python3 src/server.py), or paste a recording string. Shows your car's W/A/S/D inputs on screen, and can delete your saved record for the map.

This mod is non-vanilla: leaderboard and profile uploads are disabled while it is loaded, so replayed runs can never be submitted as your own.
